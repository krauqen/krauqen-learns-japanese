# Start Here

Use this file when you open the repo and want the next action.

## Step 1: Pick Today's Version

- Minimum: 5-10 min review, 1 tiny sentence, log the day.
- Normal: 20-30 min lesson, 10-20 min review, 10-20 min reading/game prep.
- Stretch: normal day plus 15-30 min Animal Crossing or extra reading.

## Step 2: Follow The Current Week

Open [plans/8-week-recovery-plan.md](plans/8-week-recovery-plan.md) and find the current week.

For week 1, the target is:

> Read hiragana-only words in beginner examples, simple labels, game titles, or song titles without romanization.

## Step 3: Log Evidence

Copy [logs/daily-log-template.md](logs/daily-log-template.md) into a dated file when useful, or just fill it in directly during early setup.

The log should answer one question:

> What real Japanese did I meet today?

## Step 4: Keep The Phrase Bank Alive

When a word or phrase feels game-relevant, add it to [vocab/animal-crossing-phrase-bank.md](vocab/animal-crossing-phrase-bank.md).

Do not capture everything. Capture what repeats, helps gameplay, or makes you curious enough to remember it.

## First Session

For the first session, do this:

1. Spend 10 minutes on hiragana.
2. Read 5 short hiragana words.
3. Add 3 game-relevant words to the phrase bank.
4. Fill in one daily log.

## Guided Session

Ask Codex:

```text
Use $daily-japanese-coach to walk me through today's Japanese study session and update the repo afterward.
```

Use this when you want help deciding how to spend today's available time, especially on uneven recovery days.
