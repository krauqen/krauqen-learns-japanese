## Selection Rules

- Prefer resources you will actually use while recovering.
- Prefer resources that make reading easier soon.
- Avoid adding a new resource until an old one has clearly failed or finished its job.
- Animal Crossing phrases outrank generic vocab when they repeat in real play.

## Questions To Revisit After Month 1

- Is self-study enough for accountability?
- Would an online tutor help with confusion or motivation?
- Is review too heavy, too light, or just right?
- Is Animal Crossing energizing yet, or still too frustrating?
