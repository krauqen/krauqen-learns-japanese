# 8-Week Recovery Plan

This plan assumes home recovery from ACL surgery, uneven energy, and a goal of becoming ready to play Animal Crossing: New Horizons in Japanese with support.

## Daily Versions

Use the version that fits the day. Surgery recovery counts as context, not failure.

| Version | When to use it | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum | Pain, fatigue, low focus | 5-10 min kana/vocab review, 1 tiny sentence, log the day |
| Normal | Ordinary recovery day | 20-30 min lesson, 10-20 min review, 10-20 min reading/game prep |
| Stretch | Clear head, good energy | Normal day plus 15-30 min Animal Crossing or extra reading |

## Weekly Rhythm

- 5 study days
- 1 lighter review day
- 1 flexible rest/catch-up day

Each week ends with a real-world test: something you can actually do in Japanese.

## Week 1: Read The Doorways

**Primary goal:** hiragana recognition becomes usable in the wild.

**Study focus**

- Hiragana reading and writing recognition.
- Basic pronunciation.
- Very simple sentence shape: `A は B です`.
- First particles: は, の, か.

**Real-world test**

Read hiragana-only words in beginner examples, simple labels, game titles, or song titles without romanization.

**Animal Crossing link**

Start noticing kana in screenshots or Japanese UI examples. Do not try to understand full dialogue yet.

**End-of-week evidence**

- Read a hiragana chart in shuffled order.
- Read 20 short hiragana words.
- Log 10 words that feel game-relevant.

## Week 2: Survive Japanese UI

**Primary goal:** katakana plus core interface words.

**Study focus**

- Katakana recognition.
- Common UI words: menu, save, item, option, start, cancel, yes/no.
- Particles: を, に, で.
- Basic verbs: do, go, come, see, buy, sell, make, talk.

**Real-world test**

Navigate common Japanese game/app UI words such as セーブ, メニュー, アイテム, スタート, オプション, キャンセル, はい, いいえ.

**Animal Crossing link**

Collect likely ACNH interface terms in the phrase bank.

**End-of-week evidence**

- Read shuffled katakana.
- Recognize 20 common UI words.
- Translate 5 short UI prompts with dictionary help.

## Week 3: Understand Simple Actions

**Primary goal:** understand short task-like sentences.

**Study focus**

- Verb forms: dictionary form, ます form, negative basics.
- Common action patterns: buy this, go there, talk to X, bring Y, make Z.
- Question words: what, where, who, when, why, how.

**Real-world test**

Understand simple task instructions well enough to know what action is expected.

**Animal Crossing link**

Build a mini list for daily actions: catch, fish, dig, plant, sell, buy, craft, donate, talk.

**End-of-week evidence**

- Parse 10 short action sentences.
- Create 15 action vocab entries.
- Explain 5 sentences in plain English.

## Week 4: Read Tiny Conversations

**Primary goal:** basic N5-ish grammar starts becoming usable.

**Study focus**

- い-adjectives and な-adjectives.
- Past tense basics.
- Giving and receiving basics.
- Common connectors: and, but, because.

**Real-world test**

Read a short beginner dialogue or villager-style exchange and identify who did what, even when some words need lookup.

**Animal Crossing link**

Read short Japanese snippets from ACNH screenshots, wiki examples, or captured dialogue. Focus on intent, not perfect translation.

**End-of-week evidence**

- Read one short dialogue.
- Log 20 useful phrases.
- Write a 3-sentence English summary of what happened in the dialogue.

## Month 1 Checkpoint

Use [checkpoints/month-1.md](../checkpoints/month-1.md).

The goal is not mastery. The goal is that kana, basic parsing, and dictionary lookup are no longer mysterious.

## Week 5: Start Animal Crossing With Training Wheels

**Primary goal:** begin ACNH in Japanese deliberately.

**Study focus**

- Review weak grammar from weeks 1-4.
- Common noun categories: tools, furniture, clothes, bugs, fish, fruit, money, time, locations.
- Lookup workflow: capture, dictionary, phrase bank, review.

**Real-world test**

Play 10-15 minutes in Japanese, capture 10 unknown phrases, and understand the purpose of each interaction after lookup.

**Animal Crossing link**

First controlled Japanese play sessions. Stop before frustration becomes the main memory.

**End-of-week evidence**

- 2-3 short Japanese play sessions.
- 30 captured words or phrases.
- 10 high-value phrases selected for review.

## Week 6: Handle Daily Island Routines

**Primary goal:** understand routine gameplay loops.

**Study focus**

- Shop language.
- Inventory and item descriptions.
- Crafting language.
- Announcements and daily activities.

**Real-world test**

In Japanese, handle shops, inventory, crafting, catching, selling, and basic announcements with limited lookup.

**Animal Crossing link**

Play repeated daily loops in Japanese to make common language familiar.

**End-of-week evidence**

- Complete one routine island checklist in Japanese.
- Log repeated phrases instead of every unknown phrase.
- Identify 5 grammar patterns that keep appearing.

## Week 7: Follow Villager Intent

**Primary goal:** understand the shape and tone of villager dialogue.

**Study focus**

- Requests, thanks, invitations, offers, comments.
- Casual endings and sentence-final particles at a recognition level.
- Emotional adjectives and reactions.

**Real-world test**

When a villager talks to you, usually tell whether they are joking, asking, thanking, offering, inviting, or commenting.

**Animal Crossing link**

Focus play sessions on villager interactions. Capture fewer but richer examples.

**End-of-week evidence**

- 10 villager interactions logged.
- Label each by intent.
- Save 10 reusable phrases.

## Week 8: Play A Real Session

**Primary goal:** ACNH becomes a learning environment rather than a wall of text.

**Study focus**

- Review the phrase bank.
- Revisit confusing grammar.
- Practice selective lookup: repeated, important, or interesting phrases.

**Real-world test**

Play 30 minutes in Japanese and only pause for lookups that seem important or repeated. Afterwards, write a 5-sentence English summary of what happened and save 10 useful Japanese phrases.

**Animal Crossing link**

Use the game as the main reading practice for the week.

**End-of-week evidence**

- One 30-minute Japanese play session.
- 5-sentence session summary.
- Month 2 checkpoint completed.

## Month 2 Checkpoint

Use [checkpoints/month-2.md](../checkpoints/month-2.md).

At the end of week 8, decide the next phase based on evidence:

- More foundation if parsing is still painful.
- More gameplay if routine ACNH text is becoming readable.
- Add tutor/group accountability if isolation or confusion is the main blocker.
