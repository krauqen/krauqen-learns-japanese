# 6-12 Month Roadmap

This is a compass, not a contract. Update it after the first 2 months.

## Months 1-2: Foundation And First Gameplay

**Theme:** build enough base to start using Animal Crossing as input.

**Expected outcome**

- Kana is automatic enough for real reading.
- Basic sentence parsing is possible.
- ACNH can be played in short Japanese sessions with frequent lookup.
- A phrase bank and review habit exist.

## Months 3-4: Routine Gameplay And N4 Core

**Theme:** make daily island life readable.

**Expected outcome**

- Common ACNH loops need much less lookup.
- Villager requests and routine dialogue are often understandable by intent.
- N4 grammar study fills gaps that show up in the game.
- Reading speed improves through repeated exposure.

**Possible real-world goals**

- Play 3-5 Japanese ACNH sessions per week.
- Understand daily announcements and shop interactions.
- Maintain 20-40 useful new cards or phrases per week.

## Months 5-6: Comfort Phase

**Theme:** reduce interruption and expand input.

**Expected outcome**

- ACNH feels playable in Japanese most days.
- Lookup becomes selective instead of constant.
- More jokes, personality, and flavor text come through.
- Japanese is no longer only a study activity.

**Possible real-world goals**

- Play one 60-minute Japanese session.
- Write short English or simple Japanese summaries of island events.
- Try another gentle native input source, such as beginner manga, graded readers, or another cozy game.

## Months 7-12: Broaden Beyond Animal Crossing

**Theme:** turn game fluency into broader Japanese ability.

**Expected outcome**

- Maintain ACNH as comfortable input.
- Add listening and speaking if they lag behind reading.
- Explore other games, manga, videos, or conversation practice.
- Decide whether a formal exam target, such as JLPT N4 or N3, would be motivating.

**Possible real-world goals**

- Play a second Japanese game.
- Read a short manga chapter with support.
- Hold simple tutor conversations about your island, daily life, and interests.
