## Real Japanese Encountered

Japanese: これはさかなです / これはたぬきです
Meaning: This is a fish. / This is a tanuki.
What helped me understand it: Split the sentence into これ = this, は = topic marker pronounced wa, さかな or たぬき = the thing, and です = is/am/are.
